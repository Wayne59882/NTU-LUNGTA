RECOGNITION SOURCES
-------------------
1. Curated natural-language normalization:
   V/Q variants, Chinese/English terminology, abbreviations, spacing,
   formula spellings, posture expressions, blood-flow/perfusion wording,
   disease names, dead-space/shunt families, oxygenation/ABG terminology.

2. Automatic KB-derived lexicon:
   every distinctive canonical KB concept, alias, and question pattern is
   automatically available to the recognition layer. When a distinctive
   alias is recognized, the query is enriched with that existing concept
   and its existing course anchor before the unchanged scoring pipeline.

3. Existing detection-only fuzzy tolerance:
   fuzzy matching remains in the original downstream detector and still
   contributes zero fuzzy score. Automatic KB-alias expansion itself uses
   exact/compact matching only to avoid unrelated concept injection.

4. Ambiguity guard:
   a KB alias is auto-expanded only if that exact alias maps to one unique
   canonical concept in one course. Ambiguous aliases are left to the existing
   scoring/retrieval logic instead of forcing a course.

SAFETY
------
Generic intent words and very short/generic aliases are excluded.
Posture expressions such as "站著" or "坐著" require respiratory/VQ/
perfusion context before they add V/Q/perfusion recognition terms.
